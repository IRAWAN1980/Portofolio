Background

The problem it solves

Before

  • Analysts opened a dozen portals (VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB, Shodan, EDR consoles) one by one to investigate a single IOC.
  • Abuse and RBL tickets from customer service and NOC were tracked manually in spreadsheets, with no measurable SLA.
  • Monthly XDR reports for customers were assembled by hand and took days.
  • There was no single place to see who was on duty and which alerts were still unhandled.

After

  • One scan returns a verdict, risk score, MITRE ATT&CK mapping and mitigation recommendations.
  • Abuse tickets get an automatic owner, business-hours SLA, reminders and a monthly KPI dashboard.
  • Monthly reports are generated from synchronized data, exported as PPTX, PDF and Excel.
  • A unified Alert Inbox collects alerts from every module and routes them to the analyst on duty.

Deep dive

Network Abuse team KPI & SLA

An RBL delisting ticket runs on two different clocks: the SOC team's work and the waiting time on the RBL operator. Combined, the team looks slow when it is actually waiting on a third party. What I built:

  1. Ticket state is reconstructed from the event timeline, so SLA is recalculated accurately every time it is read.
  2. Team SLA uses business hours (08:00–17:00, Mon–Fri) and pauses while waiting on the customer or once all delist requests are submitted.
  3. RBL performance is measured separately on wall-clock time against each RBL's own target.
  4. The ticket owner is picked automatically: the on-duty analyst with the lightest caseload.
  5. Automatic reminders for missed SLAs and untouched tickets, plus an automatic monthly KPI report (PDF + Excel).

Engineering practice

How the project is kept secure and reliable

Security first

Secrets live only in environment configuration. A pre-commit hook blocks keys, tokens and data files from entering the repository. Impactful EDR actions always require analyst confirmation.

Tested

64 unit test files covering scoring, SLA logic, access control, reporting and the API. Testing runs against a copy of the data, never production.

Maintainable

Scripted backup, install and restore for moving between servers. Automatic data retention and weekly maintenance keep storage under control.
